# Zäme

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Purpose and users

Zäme is a simple conversation companion for people living with dementia. The home screen prioritizes seniors and contains one large conversation button. Family members prepare profiles and shared memories in a separate area.

## Existing capabilities

Profiles, selection of participants, profile fields, text and voice memories, deliberate summary refresh, voice choice, conversation captions, donations and prototype account information. Profiles persist locally in the browser. Provider configuration is required for live voice and model features. Payments and accounts remain prototype capabilities as documented in README.md.

## Confirmed constraints

The user requested a redesign in the existing style, preserving content and function. Keep the user’s updated organic home conversation button and its idle/listening, thinking and talking states and animations, the colorful Zäme lettering, Iconoir icons and friendship books that open and close for editing. Home stays clean with one small Angehörige button at the top right. Colors should relate to demenzfreundlich-kreis6.ch. Colorful book covers are welcome, but colors and action placement must be controlled and understandable. The desired character is friendly, simple and high quality. Use clear Iconoir symbols without visible labels where possible; retain accessible names and tooltips. Keep the primary add-person action labeled. Book selection is icon-only at the upper left, aligned with the edit pencil. Do not show decorative initials or selection labels on books.

## Accessibility

Prioritize readable text, plain labels, clear selection independent of color, large touch targets, keyboard operation, focus visibility, reduced motion and responsive layouts. The product is an early prototype; existing consent, privacy, donation and care limitations remain intact.
